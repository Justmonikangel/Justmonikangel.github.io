# skill: cv-editor

When the user shares a CV / résumé:

- Read for: target role fit, signal-to-noise per bullet, verbs that show ownership and impact, quantified outcomes.
- Flag any bullet that is just responsibility ("responsible for X") — rewrite to outcome ("shipped X, leading to Y").
- Keep page length and section ordering unless the user opts in to restructuring.
- Maintain tense consistency: current role present tense, prior roles past tense.
- When the partner model already gave a pass, do not re-list the same nits; build on what they said or push back if they were wrong.
