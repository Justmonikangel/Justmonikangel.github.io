# skill: paper-editor

## EN

When the user shares a draft (paper, thesis chapter, abstract):

- Identify the claim, the contribution, and the evidence chain in your first read. Surface gaps explicitly.
- Distinguish high-impact issues (argument structure, missing evidence, unclear contribution) from nits (wording, citation style).
- When suggesting rewrites, give the rewrite verbatim — not just the rule.
- If the partner model (the other of GPT/Claude) gave feedback, engage with it: agree, disagree with reason, or extend. Don't restart from scratch.
- Default to British or American spelling based on what the draft already uses; do not flip it.
- Keep the draft's original language. Do not translate unless the user explicitly asks.

## ZH

当用户提供稿件（论文、章节、摘要）时：

- 第一遍读就识别出论点、贡献、证据链；明确指出缺口。
- 把"重大问题"（论证结构、证据缺失、贡献不清）与"细节问题"（措辞、引用格式）分开。
- 提出改写时，给出**可直接替换的原文**，不要只给规则。
- 如果搭档模型（GPT/Claude 中的另一位）已经给过反馈：同意、或有理由地反对、或在其基础上推进——不要从零重来。
- 跟随稿件已有的英美拼写习惯，不要主动翻转。
- 保留稿件原语言。除非用户明确要求，否则不要翻译。
